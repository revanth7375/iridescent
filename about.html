The Origin

How IRIDESCENT
Came to Be

IRIDESCENT was born in Hyderabad in 2020 when our founder, Riya Desai, found herself standing in boutique after boutique, unable to find a dress that matched the vision in her mind.

Instead of giving up, she hired a tailor and spent three weeks describing, sketching, and collaborating until the dress in her imagination existed in the real world.

"If I had to work this hard just to wear what I could see in my mind, I knew something had to change."

That experience became IRIDESCENT — a space where imagination is treated as the starting point, not the obstacle.

The Philosophy

What We Believe

We believe that every person carries an image of themselves they've never quite been able to dress for. A vision of who they are at their most radiant, their most themselves.

Our job is to find that image — through conversation, through listening, through the kind of creative partnership that great tailors have always offered — and bring it to life.

"A garment that was made for no one else in the world feels entirely different when you put it on."

Every piece we create exists only once. That's the IRIDESCENT promise.

What We Stand For

Our Values

✦
Originality Above All

We never duplicate a design. Every IRIDESCENT piece is created once and belongs exclusively to the client who inspired it. Your dress will never be worn by a stranger.

◈
Deep Listening

Our consultations are not sales meetings. They are creative conversations where we ask more than we speak — because the dress lives in your words first.

❧
Sustainable Craftsmanship

We source ethically, produce slowly, and build garments that last. In a world of fast fashion, we are proudly, deliberately slow — and every stitch reflects that care.

⊹
Inclusivity by Design

Bespoke means made for you — your body, your story, your size. We celebrate every form and have never turned away a client for any reason of size, shape, or background.
