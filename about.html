The Origin

An Idea
That became IRIDESCENT

Iridescent was founded by Rachana Talasila with a vision to create timeless pieces inspired by nature, individuality, and refined craftsmanship. Rooted in minimalism and elegance, the brand blends heritage artistry with contemporary design to create pieces that feel personal, meaningful, and enduring.

Inspired by the glow of sunrise, soft hues of dawn, and the beauty of simplicity, every Iridescent creation is thoughtfully crafted to celebrate individuality and timeless luxury — a true celebration of you.

"Iridescent was never just about clothing — it was about creating pieces that make people feel seen, confident, and celebrated."

The Philosophy

What We Believe

Our commitment goes beyond aesthetics—we believe in the transformative power of clothing to elevate not just appearance, but spirit. Every piece we create is meticulously crafted with care, passion, and a focus on sustainability, ensuring it stands as a symbol of quality, integrity, and conscious luxury

"We believe that clothing should be more than just a statement"

—it should be a celebration of who you are, enhancing your inner radiance and inspiring you to shine brighter every day..

What We Stand For

Our Values

✦
Originality Above All

We never duplicate a design. Every IRIDESCENT piece is created once and belongs exclusively to the client who inspired it. Your dress will never be worn by a stranger.

◈
Deep Listening

Our consultations are not sales meetings. They are creative conversations where we ask more than we speak — because the dress lives in your words first.

❧
Sustainable Craftsmanship

We source ethically, produce slowly, and build garments that last. In a world of fast fashion, we are proudly, deliberately slow — and every stitch reflects that care.

⊹
Inclusivity by Design

Bespoke means made for you — your body, your story, your size. We celebrate every form and have never turned away a client for any reason of size, shape, or background.
